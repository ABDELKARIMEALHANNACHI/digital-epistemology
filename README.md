# Digital Epistemology

**Cybersecurity × Philosophy × Systems Thinking**

*Question what you see. Investigate what you cannot see.*

## Purpose

A personal investigation into how we understand digital systems,
question their assumptions, construct mental models, and establish
claims through evidence.

This is a research notebook, not merely a collection of tools or tutorials.

## The 30-Day Ritual

Thirty daily conversations exploring the hidden architecture of the
internet through analogies, technical analysis, philosophical inquiry,
and controlled experiments.

## Research Principles

- Observation is not explanation.
- An anomaly is not automatically a vulnerability.
- A hypothesis is not proof.
- Test assumptions rather than trusting appearances.
- Record uncertainty, failed hypotheses, and contradictory evidence.
- Conduct security experiments only on authorized systems.

## Repository Structure

- `00-foundations/` — purpose, principles, and methodology
- `01-daily-ritual/` — the 30-day investigation
- `02-mental-models/` — reusable ways of reasoning

- `07-reflections/` — lessons, questions, and changing perspectives

## Core Question

How do we know what we think we know about a digital system?
