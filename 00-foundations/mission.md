# Mission

To develop a deeper understanding of digital systems by combining
cybersecurity, philosophy, systems thinking, adversarial reasoning,
and disciplined experimentation.

The goal is not simply to learn how systems work, but to understand
what they guarantee, what they assume, where their boundaries lie,
and how those claims can be tested.

## Guiding Question

What can I observe, what can I infer, and what can I actually prove?
